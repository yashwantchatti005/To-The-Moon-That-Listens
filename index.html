01 The Book

A book for

the feelings that stayed.

Some feelings arrive as words. Others arrive as silence.

To the Moon That Listens is a debut collection of poems and fragments shaped by love, memory, distance, loss, healing, identity and the quiet search for home within ourselves.

The book borrows its language from nature and silence — from moments that leave their trace quietly, and from feelings that do not always ask to be understood.

From the book
“Somewhere between the words, you may find yourself.”

A quiet invitation

The words will remain the same. What changes is what you notice.

Read slowly. Come back when you need to.

The Writer

Vijaya Suriya

is a poet and writer whose work demonstrates restraint, coherence, and attentiveness to emotional nuance.

Through minimal language and natural imagery, her poems examine relationships, memory, and inner transformation.

To the Moon That Listens is her debut poetry collection.
