01 The Book

A book for

the feelings that stayed.

Some feelings arrive as words. Others arrive as silence.

To the Moon That Listens is a debut collection of poems and fragments shaped by love, memory, distance, loss, healing, identity and the quiet search for home within ourselves.

The book borrows its language from nature and silence — from moments that leave their trace quietly, and from feelings that do not always ask to be understood.

From the book
“Not everything asks to make sense.
Some things are simply carried forward.”

A quiet invitation

Some pages may feel familiar. Others may remain just out of reach.

Read slowly. Come back when you need to.

02 Selected Excerpts

Five moments from the collection

Words that stay.

01 Love

“I didn't fall for you.
I leaned.
Slowly.
Trusting the ground
would stay.”

02 Letting Go

“I can never unlove you,
but I can unweave myself further away.”

03 Healing

“Maybe the scars do remain forever.
We simply learn to grow around them.”

04 Hope

“If all you did was get through today,
that is enough.
You are seen.”

05 The Moon

“The moon never explains itself.
Even on days with no light,
it simply shows up.”
